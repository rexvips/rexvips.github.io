Ready to challenge yourself? These intermediate words will help you express more complex ideas and emotions. Master these to take your communication to the next level!

---

## 1. Ambiguous
**Category:** Intermediate  
**Meaning:** Open to more than one interpretation; unclear or vague

**Short Conversation:**
> "The instructions were quite ambiguous - I'm not sure what they want."  
> "Let me read them again. Maybe we can figure it out together."

**Similar Words:** Unclear, vague, confusing, uncertain, indefinite

## Learning Strategies for Intermediate Vocabulary

### Context Clues
When you encounter unfamiliar words, look for:
- **Definition clues:** The meaning is explained directly
- **Example clues:** Examples help clarify meaning
- **Contrast clues:** Opposite words provide hints
- **Synonym clues:** Similar words appear nearby

### Word Families
Group words by their roots:
- **Spect** (to look): inspect, respect, spectacular
- **Dict** (to say): predict, dictate, contradict
- **Port** (to carry): transport, portable, import

### Practice Exercises
1. **Synonym replacement:** Replace simple words with intermediate vocabulary
2. **Context writing:** Create paragraphs using 3-4 new words
3. **Word association:** Connect new words to personal experiences
4. **Debate practice:** Use sophisticated vocabulary in discussions

### Memory Techniques
- **Visualization:** Create mental images for abstract concepts
- **Personal connections:** Relate words to your own experiences
- **Word stories:** Create narratives incorporating new vocabulary
- **Spaced repetition:** Review words at increasing intervals

Remember: Intermediate vocabulary opens doors to more nuanced communication and deeper understanding!
