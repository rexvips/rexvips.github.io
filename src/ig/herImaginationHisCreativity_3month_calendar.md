# HerImaginationHisCreativity - Instagram Growth Strategy & 3-Month Content Calendar

## Account Audit & Analysis

### Current State (November 2024)
- **Followers:** 450
- **Weekly Reach:** 1,000 impressions
- **Engagement Rate:** Low (estimated 1-2%)
- **Top Performing Content:** Reels

### Strengths
- Couple dynamic provides authentic dual perspective content
- Toronto location offers rich local content opportunities
- Reels performance shows video content resonates
- Lifestyle niche allows for diverse content pillars

### Weaknesses
- Low engagement rate indicates content not connecting with audience
- Small follower base limits organic reach
- Inconsistent posting likely affecting algorithm performance
- Missing local Toronto community connections

### Key Opportunities
1. **Toronto Seasonal Content:** Leverage changing seasons, festivals, and local events
2. **Couple Storytelling:** Behind-the-scenes relationship content performs well
3. **Local Food Scene:** Toronto's diverse culinary landscape
4. **Neighbourhood Spotlights:** Different Toronto areas offer unique content
5. **Home & Lifestyle:** Cozy couple living content trending

## Target Audience Definition

### Primary Demographics
- **Age:** 25-35 years old
- **Gender:** 65% female, 35% male
- **Location:** Toronto GTA, Ontario, Canada
- **Income:** $45k-$85k CAD
- **Relationship Status:** In relationships, couples, newlyweds

### Psychographics
- Values authentic, real-life content over polished perfection
- Interested in local experiences and hidden gems
- Seeks relationship inspiration and couple goals
- Enjoys seasonal activities and Toronto lifestyle
- Active on Instagram Stories and Reels consumption

### Priority Toronto Neighbourhoods
1. **King West** - Young professionals, trendy restaurants
2. **Queen West** - Creative community, unique shops
3. **Distillery District** - Date nights, seasonal events
4. **Kensington Market** - Eclectic food, vintage shopping
5. **Liberty Village** - Young couples, modern living
6. **Junction Triangle** - Emerging neighbourhood, local cafes
